Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

What I collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, I collect the name, phone number, email address, package selection and message you provide. No other personal data is collected automatically — this site does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Why I collect it

Solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and communicate with you about your project. Your details are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Where it's stored

Submissions are stored securely in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), encrypted in transit. Only I have access to this data — it cannot be read, listed, or exported by site visitors.

Your rights (POPIA)

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information I hold about you. To do so, email elevatewebdesigns26@gmail.com and I'll action your request within a reasonable time.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact me directly on elevatewebdesigns26@gmail.com or WhatsApp.
