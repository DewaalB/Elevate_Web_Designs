Questions about your SEO score

What is a good SEO score?

Above 90 means the technical foundations are in great shape. Between 70 and 90 is solid, with quick wins available. Below 70 usually means real issues are stopping Google from showing your website to people searching for what you offer.

Does a high score guarantee I'll rank first on Google?

No. The score measures whether your website gives Google what it needs: clear titles, fast secure pages, readable content and correct business details. Rankings also depend on competition, reviews and how long your site has been around, but fixing these basics is the step you control.

Why does local SEO matter for my business?

Most customers search for services near them, like "plumber in Bloemfontein" or "web designer near me". Google decides who appears in those results and on the map partly from the business details on your website, so a complete phone number, address and business markup make a real difference.
